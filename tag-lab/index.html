TAG LAB

Steer your defender (drag/hold or WASD/Arrows) against the attacker. Arms make the tag automatically in range.

  • 1. Quick Press: Sprint up on GO to reduce the space they have to attack.
  • 2. Stutter & Pace: Brake before contact, then move back with the attacker to minimize relative speed.
  • Avoid Sprinting Past: Swiping at high relative speed misses the tag, and you cannot outrun them from behind.
